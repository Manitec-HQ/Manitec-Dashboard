# Experiment Queue Template

## Status

**Planned** — create a reusable experiment-queue template for ONE and Manitec research work.

## Required Template Fields

- Experiment title and research question
- Hypothesis and decision it informs
- Owner and status
- System/version/provider under test
- Scope, fixtures, and inputs
- Constraints and security/data-handling boundaries
- Success criteria and failure criteria
- Required output artifacts and trace locations
- Evaluation method, human scoring rubric, and baseline
- Quality, task-success, latency, token/cost, and reliability measurements
- Findings, decision, follow-up, and evidence links

## Definition of Done

The template is usable for small tool probes and larger agent-system benchmarks, produces comparable records across runs, and makes adoption/defer/reject decisions auditable.
